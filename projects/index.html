Cannabis Fertigation Based on Sensor Data

2022 • Cannabis Science Conference, Long Beach, California

Project Description & Application

Developed a sensor-based approach to irrigation and fertigation management for cannabis cultivation. The project examined the use of sensor data to manage water availability and controlled water stress during the final growth stage, where water management may influence crop value and THC content. The work also examined water distribution within small growing containers. The approach has potential applications beyond cannabis, including flowers, ornamentals, and greenhouse crops.

Commercial Application

Sales of monitoring systems that can help growers improve crop yield and crop value through data-driven water management.

Soil Water Drainage Assessment Using Fast Fourier Transform

2021–2022 • Midwest U.S. stakeholders

Project Description & Application

Developed a Fast Fourier Transform algorithm for analyzing time-series soil moisture data to identify drainage and over-irrigation at multiple soil depths. The work addressed agricultural runoff associated with dairy and swine operations and fertigation of row crops, with the objective of demonstrating water distribution without excessive drainage and supporting regulatory applications.

Commercial Application

Sales of monitoring stations with soil sensors to fertigation delivery system manufacturers.

New Machine Learning Method for Determining Soil Salinity Using Time Domain Reflectometry (TDR)

2023 • Stakeholder Research

Project Description & Application

Developed a machine-learning method for estimating soil salinity from electrical signals generated by Time Domain Reflectometry. Python was used to isolate key signal features correlated with soil properties, which were then evaluated using machine-learning regressors and training data. The work extends TDR applications beyond soil moisture measurement to soil salinity and has applications in agriculture, geotechnical monitoring, and environmental assessment.

Commercial Application

Sales of Time Domain Reflectometry soil sensors.

Deployment of Landslide Risk Detection Systems in SE Asia

2025 • Stakeholder Research

Project Description & Application

Examined real-time soil hydraulic monitoring for landslide risk detection. Soil hydraulic head can serve as a dynamic parameter related to water content and the water table, while soil stability represents a more static component of slope behavior. The project investigated hill-slope monitoring for real-time "now-cast" warning systems and applications for governments addressing landslide risk in Southeast Asia.

Commercial Application

Sales of monitoring stations with soil sensors and cloud services to distributors and government safety programs.

Method for Determining Plant Water Stress in Stormwater Runoff Rain Gardens

2022 • Municipal Stakeholders

Project Description & Application

Developed a sensor-based method for identifying plant water stress in stormwater rain gardens. Rain gardens are designed to filter runoff, but summer plant stress can require municipalities to use water trucks for irrigation. Time-series soil moisture measurements can be used to identify stress thresholds and support data-driven irrigation decisions with the potential to reduce water use.

Commercial Water Retention Curve Laboratory for ASTM D6836-16 Using Real-Time Monitoring

2025–2026 • EuroSoil, Seville, Spain • EGU • Other Stakeholders

Project Description & Application

Developed and piloted a commercial laboratory approach for measuring soil water retention curves using real-time monitoring. Soil water retention curves describe the relationship between water content and the pressure required to remove water from soil. Applications include irrigation, drainage, water supply, landslide assessment, flood management, and wildfire research. Sensors placed inside soil samples during pressurization can provide real-time measurements, potentially reducing testing time and error while making the resulting data more useful for commercial applications.

Commercial Application

Sales of soil sensors, soil water retention curve laboratory equipment, and testing services to governmental water-resource authorities.
